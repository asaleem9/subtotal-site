# Subtotal site brief

Interviewed via plan-mode questions on 2026-10-15 (structure, CTA, hosting, artwork answered by
Ali); the remaining topics are self-authored under that direction.

1. **Vibe:** printed receipt, rubber stamp, carbon copy, sleepy octopus. References: a thermal
   receipt printer, a Muji ledger, the app itself.
2. **Journey:** meet Octo → the stuff you forgot you pay for → how adding works (two taps) → what
   it costs you (nothing) and what it doesn't do → build your own ledger → the carbon copy
   (dark mode, iPhone Duo, iPad) → TOTAL, tear-off, stamp.
3. **Energy:** warm open, tightening, release at the clip, quiet, peak, cool, resolved.
4. **Feeling curve** (emotion, then cause):
   1. Delight: Octo holding real logos in six arms, planes separating under the pointer.
   2. Unease: line items printing one by one onto the tape, the subtotal climbing.
   3. Relief: the phone adds Netflix in two taps while the wheel scrubs the clip.
   4. Trust: plain facts as receipt rows. $0.00 for the app, "none" for bank linking.
   5. Agency (peak): you type "spot", pick Spotify, and the tape re-prints with your number.
   6. Range: the same ledger as a carbon copy, on a folded Duo and an open iPad, moving sideways.
   7. Resolve: TOTAL prints, the receipt tears along the perforation, the red stamp lands.
   **Peak:** act 5. "I typed in the stuff I pay for and an octopus printed me a receipt."
5. **One thing no site does:** the running tape. A fixed receipt that accumulates as you pass
   claims, becomes *your* receipt at the peak, and is the nav.
6. **Range:** editorial-playful. Paper, ink, one stamp red. Not premium-minimal.
7. **Structure:** distinct line items on one sheet (Ali: "One long receipt"). Not a world flight.
8. **Assets:** 9 Octo poses, app icon, 12 App Store screenshots, 587-service catalog with ~500
   logos, the app's Palette/Typography tokens. Two new poses via Codex (octo-write, octo-printer).

**Tell-someone:** it's the site where you build your own subscription receipt and it adds up
while you scroll.

**Authored silence:** act 4 (flow, quiet facts) is deliberately the quietest screen before the
peak. Nothing else on the page is meant to be empty.

**Grammar:** the printed receipt (new). Unit = line item; chrome = the tape; hero = receipt
header + layered Octo; ending = TOTAL, tear, stamp. Bans: scrub beyond one act, dark scrims,
crossfading pinned type stacks, magnet, card grids, centred copy throughout, drift.

**Signature move:** the running tape + build-your-ledger picker (page JS, engine untouched).
